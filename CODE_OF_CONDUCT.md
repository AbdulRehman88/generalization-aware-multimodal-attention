# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free collaboration environment. Contributors must communicate professionally, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy and research-ethics restrictions, and avoid publishing participant-level or confidential material.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and repeated disruption of technical collaboration.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, participants, and the integrity of the project.
